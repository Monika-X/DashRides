Privacy & Trust

Your Privacy Is Non-Negotiable

Effective Date: October 1, 2026 • Verified compliance with EU GDPR, California CCPA, and ISO/IEC 27001 Information Security standards.

✓ Zero Data Monetization ✓ 256-Bit Encrypted Telemetry ✓ Automated 30-Day Purge
Section 01

1. Vehicle GPS & Telemetry Data Processing

When you operate a DashRides vehicle, our hardware transmits coordinates, velocity, battery voltage, and brake status to calculate trip duration, enforce municipal geofenced speed limits (e.g. 15 km/h in pedestrian plazas), and locate scooters for battery maintenance.

Anonymization Guarantee: Raw GPS breadcrumbs are detached from your personal user account after 72 hours and converted into aggregated traffic flow heatmaps used solely for fleet rebalancing. We never sell your travel routes to advertising networks or third-party data brokers.

Section 02

2. Payment Tokenization & License Verification

All financial transactions, security deposits, and rental authorizations are processed through PCI-DSS Level 1 certified processors (Stripe & Apple Pay). DashRides never stores raw credit card numbers or CVV codes on our servers.

Driver's license images uploaded for moped verification are verified instantaneously via cryptographic biometric matching and encrypted at rest with AES-256 keys.

Section 03

3. Third-Party Integrations & Legal Compliance

We only share minimal necessary data with verified service partners under strict Data Processing Agreements (DPAs):

  • Insurance Underwriters: Anonymized trip timestamps in the event of a reported collision or liability claim.
  • Municipal Transport Authorities: General aggregate fleet counts via standardized Mobility Data Specification (MDS) APIs.
  • Emergency Responders: Precise GPS coordinates in the event of an active SOS trigger or severe impact detection.
Section 04

4. Your Rights: Access, Export & Full Deletion

You retain complete control over your personal data. Under GDPR and CCPA, you have the right to:

  • Request a complete JSON/CSV export of all your rental receipts, ride logs, and invoices.
  • Opt out of marketing communications and promotional emails with one click.
  • Request permanent deletion of your account and identification records within 30 business days.
Section 05

5. Contact Our Data Protection Officer (DPO)

If you have questions, regulatory inquiries, or wish to exercise your data privacy rights, please reach out to our legal department:

Email: privacy@dashrides.com
Mailing Address: DashRides Mobility Inc., Attn: Privacy Officer, 742 Grand Avenue, Suite 100, San Francisco, CA 94107
