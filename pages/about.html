Our Manifesto

Cities Belong To The People, Not The Gridlock.

Founded by aerospace engineers and industrial designers, DashRides was built on a single obsession: replacing multi-ton fossil vehicles with precision electric micro-mobility that feels exhilarating, safe, and effortlessly accessible to everyone.

Origin & Craft

From A Small Garage Lab to 12 Metropolises

In 2021, we grew frustrated with fragile shared kick-scooters littering sidewalks and breaking after a few weeks. We knew cities deserved better: vehicles engineered to aeronautical standards with unibody aluminum alloy frames, swappable high-capacity batteries, and intelligent telemetry.

Today, DashRides operates over 5,000 custom-designed electric mopeds and scooters across major metropolitan cores, powering over 4.2 million clean commutes.
