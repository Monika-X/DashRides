Frequently Asked Support Questions

What happens if my battery runs low during a rental?

You can either visit any of our 520+ PowerSwap kiosks for a free 15-second battery exchange or tap "Request Mobile Swap" in your dashboard to have our support van meet you.

How do I report a flat tire or mechanical issue?

Tap "Report Issue" in your dashboard or call our 24/7 hotline. We will instantly unlock an adjacent scooter for you with zero transfer fees.

Are there group bookings for corporate events or bachelor parties?

Yes! We offer group rentals up to 30 scooters with dedicated tour guides and support escort vans. Email partnerships@dashrides.com for group discounts.
