Transparent Policies

Rental Rules, Deposits & Safety Guidelines

Clear terms designed to protect you, your security deposit, and our shared urban infrastructure.

How does the security deposit work and when is it refunded?
▾

Depending on the vehicle model (Scooters: $80–$100, Mopeds: $120–$150), a temporary authorization hold is placed on your credit or debit card when your booking starts. Once you park in an authorized green zone and submit the end-ride photo, the full hold is automatically released within 15–30 minutes by our automated payment gateway.

What driver's license or age requirements apply?
▾

For our Electric Scooters (Phantom Pro, CyberScoot, Urban Glide), riders must be 18+ with any valid government photo ID. For our Classic 50 and Cruiser GT EV Mopeds, riders must be 18+ with a valid standard automobile or motorcycle driver's license uploaded via the customer dashboard.

What does the $1,000,000 Insurance Policy cover?
▾

Every active rental includes third-party liability coverage up to $1,000,000 for bodily injury or third-party property damage. When opting into our $2/hr Damage Waiver, accidental cosmetic scuffs, flat tires, and accidental vehicle damages carry a $0 deductible.

Where am I allowed to ride and park?
▾

Ride in designated bicycle lanes, shared transit corridors, and roadways with speed limits up to 35 mph. Riding on pedestrian sidewalks is prohibited by municipal regulations. Park upright in any designated green zone, bike rack, or partnered parking hub.
