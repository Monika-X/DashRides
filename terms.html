Rental Master Agreement

Terms & Conditions of Rental Service

Please read this binding agreement carefully before unlocking or operating any vehicle in the DashRides global fleet.

Clause 01

1. Rider Eligibility & Verification Standards

By creating an account or unlocking a vehicle, you represent and warrant that you are at least 18 years of age, physically capable of operating a two-wheeled motorized vehicle, and possess a valid government-issued photo ID. For moped models exceeding 32 km/h, a valid automobile or motorcycle driver's license is legally required.

Clause 02

2. Billing, Security Deposits & Automatic Returns

Rental fares accrue on an hourly, daily, or monthly basis as defined in our official pricing schedule. A pre-authorization hold ($80–$150) is placed on your payment method upon trip commencement.

The deposit will be returned in full within 30 minutes following the successful termination of your rental in an authorized green zone, provided the end-of-ride inspection photo verifies no structural damage, vandalism, or improper abandonment.

Clause 03

3. Operating Conduct & Prohibited Uses

You agree to strictly adhere to the following safety rules:

  • Zero Sidewalk Riding: Vehicles must be operated in bike lanes or public roadways in compliance with local municipal codes.
  • No Tandem Riding: Scooters are engineered for single-rider use only. Exceeding the 130kg payload capacity is strictly prohibited.
  • Sobriety Mandate: Operating any vehicle under the influence of alcohol, cannabis, or controlled substances results in immediate account termination and permanent ban.
  • Helmets: Wearing an approved DOT/CE certified helmet is mandatory on all moped rentals and strongly recommended on all scooters.
Clause 04

4. Assumption of Risk, Liability & Insurance Coverage

Operating micro-mobility vehicles entails inherent risks of traffic, road surface irregularities, and weather hazards. You voluntarily assume all risks associated with your rental.

DashRides provides primary third-party liability insurance up to $1,000,000 during active trips. Personal property damage and intentional misconduct are not covered by our primary insurer.

Clause 05

5. Binding Arbitration & Class Action Waiver

Any dispute, claim, or controversy arising out of or relating to this Agreement shall be resolved through confidential, binding arbitration administered by the American Arbitration Association (AAA) in San Francisco, California, rather than in a court of law.
